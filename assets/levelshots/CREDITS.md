# Level select pictures

- Story levels (streets ... laststand): rendered from the game itself (the 3D renderer).
- Football Tour grounds (theden, stamfordbridge, anfield, oldtrafford, ibrox): 3D models built in Blender
  (scripts/blender/stadium.py, scripts/build_levelshots.py) from these reference photos on Wikimedia Commons:
  - Stamford Bridge, Anfield, Old Trafford: drone photos by Arne Müseler (arne-mueseler.com), CC BY-SA 3.0 DE
  - Ibrox Stadium: "Ibrox Stadium from the air" by Thomas Nugent (geograph.org.uk), CC BY-SA 2.0
  - The Den: "TheDen2019" panorama by TheLostBoy, CC BY-SA 4.0
